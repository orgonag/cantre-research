# Acceptance-test specification

This is a test specification. The third column names the synthetic scenario replay in
`packages/core/test/scenarios/` (milestone M3) and, from milestone M4, the Playwright spec in
`app/e2e/` that exercises a case, where one exists; every other row is still a specification, not
a claim of executed tests. The replays and the browser tests run on synthetic networks and the
committed golden tiles with mocked GPS in headless Chromium (not WebKit, not a real phone), not
field traces, and say nothing about field accuracy.
Use independently authored synthetic fixtures first. Fix numeric tolerances according
to the chosen representation and units; document them separately from field accuracy.

| Case | Required assertion | Scenario replay (synthetic, M3) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Straight track | Endpoint anchors reproduce their mileposts; interior distance fractions interpolate correctly. | None yet. |
| Curved polyline | An asymmetric bent path uses sum of segment lengths, not endpoint chord distance; projection on each segment gives expected progress. | None yet. |
| Geometry density | Coarse and dense samples of a known synthetic arc quantify approximation effects; no claim that densification recovers missing real geometry. | None yet. |
| Decreasing MPs | Increasing path distance can decrease milepost; endpoint and interior values remain correct. | None yet. |
| Projection bounds | Fix before/after a segment clamps locally; outside the outer calibration bracket returns uncalibrated rather than extrapolated MP. | None yet. |
| Bad geometry | Empty/single-point paths, duplicate vertices, zero total length and nonfinite coordinates fail or handle explicitly without NaN leaking as a usable MP. | None yet (unit tests only: zero-length and single-vertex tracks are point candidates; the decoder rejects decreasing `sMm`, out-of-envelope vertices and invalid intervals). |
| Anchors | Missing/unknown units, sentinels and unknown qualifiers rejected; valid zero retained; duplicate positions and conflicting/discontinuous mileage flagged. | None yet. |
| Units | Known mile/kilometre inputs convert consistently; output units cannot be inferred silently. | None yet. |
| Junction | Near branch point retain plausible branches until evidence resolves them; avoid an unsupported jump in subdivision/MP. | [`junction.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/junction.test.ts): five variants (same subdivision; different subdivisions cold to M, cold to Br, and approached on M, which moves to Br at once because a hold never crosses railways; same railway with the bounded hold re-selected at exactly 5000 ms); the no-milepost-jump check runs in two of them (same subdivision, different-subdivision approach). |
| Diamond/perpendicular crossing | At the intersection, equal residuals remain ambiguous without adequate direction/history; geometric crossing alone creates no connectivity. Test approach, standstill, noisy heading and departure on both routes. | [`diamond.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/diamond.test.ts): approach, standstill with a stale heading, departure on both routes; [`golden-tiles.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/golden-tiles.test.ts): the golden diamond. A heading that contradicts the only track is covered too. Noisy heading beyond that is not. In the browser, [`flows.spec.ts`](../app/e2e/flows.spec.ts) shows a fix at the synthetic diamond as `AMBIGUOUS_ROUTE` with `DIAMOND_NEARBY` and no milepost; the synthetic diamond has only one route in its data, so a two-route diamond chooser is not tested in a browser. |
| Different nearby Class I railways | Preserve separate stable route identities; closest or main-track preference alone cannot force a selection. Show source-supported labels and explicit choice when unresolved. | [`parallel-class1.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/parallel-class1.test.ts): two operators 12 m apart, then diverging; continuity keeps both routes in `candidates` and never holds one railway against the other; ambiguous, lock, unlock returns to `AMBIGUOUS_ROUTE`; [`diamond.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/diamond.test.ts): different subdivisions and operators. Source-supported labels are M4. |
| Unique candidate | Automatic selection requires validated evidence, freshness and coverage checks; an incomplete bundle returning one record must not masquerade as certainty. | [`coverage.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/coverage.test.ts): a sole candidate at a tile edge is `AMBIGUOUS_ROUTE` (`PARTIAL_COVERAGE`); [`golden-tiles.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/golden-tiles.test.ts). Freshness is in lifecycle. In the browser, [`flows.spec.ts`](../app/e2e/flows.spec.ts): a fix at the outer edge of the coverage is `AMBIGUOUS_ROUTE` shown as partial coverage, with no milepost. |
| User route/subdivision lock | Explicit choice constrains candidates and stays visible; a subdivision lock may leave track ambiguity. No silent switching at a diamond, conflicting fix or dataset update. | [`diamond.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/diamond.test.ts): a chain lock stays `MATCHED` through a diamond, then `LOCK_CONFLICT`; [`double-track.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/double-track.test.ts): a chain lock inside a subdivision; [`parallel-class1.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/parallel-class1.test.ts): unlock never leaves the locked route as an automatic match. A subdivision lock is covered by unit tests only. In the browser, [`flows.spec.ts`](../app/e2e/flows.spec.ts) covers a chain lock chosen from the chooser at the diamond and at the border, Unlock returning to the chooser, the lock cleared by a reload (with a one-time notice), and the lock cleared by a new `datasetVersion` (with a visible notice). |
| Lock conflict / stale fix | Keep the visible choice, withhold current matching when unsupported, label historical output, and require explicit change/unlock to consider another route. Newer fixes cannot be replaced by older ones. | [`diamond.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/diamond.test.ts): conflict keeps the lock and shows no milepost, only unlock clears it; [`lifecycle.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/lifecycle.test.ts): `STALE` with history, older fixes ignored, no continuity across the gap. A dataset update is covered in the app by [`flows.spec.ts`](../app/e2e/flows.spec.ts) (new `datasetVersion` clears the lock with a visible notice; a reload clears it with a notice; Unlock stays available on a conflict). |
| Choice versus precision | User selection changes candidate scope but never reduces device-reported accuracy radius or implies surveyed certainty. | [`diamond.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/diamond.test.ts) and [`double-track.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/double-track.test.ts): `accuracyM` is unchanged by a lock. In the browser, [`flows.spec.ts`](../app/e2e/flows.spec.ts): after choosing a route at the diamond the card still shows the device accuracy unchanged ("GPS accuracy ± 5 m"). |
| Parallel tracks | Equidistant and same-heading tracks yield ambiguity; noisy fixes do not fabricate certainty; continuity cannot override strong contrary evidence indefinitely. | [`double-track.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/double-track.test.ts): tracks 5 m apart, range, uncalibrated and disagreeing mileposts; [`parallel-class1.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/parallel-class1.test.ts): noisy fixes; [`junction.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/junction.test.ts): the same-railway continuity hold ending at exactly 5000 ms. Two passes of one chain (hairpin) are covered by unit tests only. |
| Gaps | Disconnected geometry and missing calibration remain gaps; never create a straight connector or mileage through missing topology. | [`golden-tiles.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/golden-tiles.test.ts): a fix between two intervals has no milepost (`DISCONTINUITY_GAP`). Disconnected topology is not exercised by a scenario. |
| Identity | Shared subdivision names do not merge distinct IDs; asset joins require valid stable references. | None yet. |
| Assets | Bridges/crossings without source MPs remain explicitly uncalibrated unless a calculated MP is labelled as derived; culverts are not bridges; duplicate/grouped records not counted as distinct assets. | None yet. |
| GPS lifecycle | Permission denied/unavailable, old or out-of-order fixes, background/resume and missing heading/speed have explicit states. Stale fixes do not appear current. | [`lifecycle.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/lifecycle.test.ts): denied, error, ignored and out-of-order fixes, stale, re-select, future-stamped fix. Background/resume is not covered by a core replay; the app's [`flows.spec.ts`](../app/e2e/flows.spec.ts) shows a fix that stops for 16 s as `STALE`, labelled historical, and denied permission as `NO_PERMISSION`. |
| Uncertainty | Display fix accuracy, source limits, cross-track residual and ambiguity separately; no invented confidence interval or survey guarantee. | Partly: `accuracyM` is asserted unchanged in [`double-track.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/double-track.test.ts). The band excludes residual and survey accuracy by design (README). In the browser, [`flows.spec.ts`](../app/e2e/flows.spec.ts) asserts that a matched card labels the band "from GPS accuracy only" and shows no confidence, probability or percentage text. |
| Reproducibility | Identical pinned input and transform configuration yield identical normalized results/hashes; corrupt input fails validation. | None yet. |
| Calculation versus display | Simplifying display tiles cannot silently change calculation geometry or mileposts; validate any deliberate calculation precision conversion. | None yet. |
| Regional boundary | Buffered bundles preserve needed route/anchor continuity; missing adjacent data produces a visible gap, not a route switch. Mixed versions are detected. | [`golden-tiles.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/golden-tiles.test.ts): the golden diamond walk crosses a shared tile edge on one chain with continuous `s` (the other golden walk stays inside one tile); [`coverage.test.ts`](../packages/core/test/scenarios/coverage.test.ts): partial and missing coverage. Mixed versions: `network-decode.test.ts`. In the browser, [`flows.spec.ts`](../app/e2e/flows.spec.ts): a device first seen at the border between two provinces' chains has both as candidates, shows no milepost and bridges nothing; the national snapshot keeps each province's chain separate. |
| Privacy | No raw GPS fix or trace leaves the device or persists without explicit opt-in. Disclose approximate location revealed by regional bundle requests before enabling delivery; browser tests inspect requests and storage. | Playwright against the built app, on the synthetic network (headless Chromium, mocked geolocation; not field tests, and not WebKit or Firefox): [`privacy.spec.ts`](../app/e2e/privacy.spec.ts) and every flow in [`flows.spec.ts`](../app/e2e/flows.spec.ts) record all requests (only the app's files, the manifest, the snapshot and the asset-details file; no coordinate in any URL, body or header; the basemap host only after consent), and inspect localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies, IndexedDB and Cache Storage afterwards. |

For interpolation tests, include exact anchor positions and uneven segment lengths;
a symmetric halfway example alone can accidentally pass chord interpolation.
For stateful matching, replay sequences through switches, reversal, standstill and
GPS jumps. Define freshness/off-track thresholds before testing their boundary cases.

## Evaluation reporting

Publish sample counts, source vintage, filtering/exclusions and error distributions.
Report held-out internal consistency separately from independently surveyed accuracy.
Reproducing the historical 57.91 m mean is not a required success condition; this repo
contains no private fixture to reproduce it. Pending 1% semantics blocks a 1% verdict,
not synthetic-core development. Field acceptance requires an agreed denominator,
near-zero handling, absolute bound and independent evidence; thresholds remain TBD.
